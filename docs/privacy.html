Privacy Policy

Tarajuu · Last updated 5 November 2026

Tarajuu compares product prices (Amazon, Flipkart) and ride fares (Uber, Rapido, Ola) in India. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. We do not sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites.

What we collect

DataWhenWhy
Mobile numberWhen you sign in with OTPTo create and secure your account. Verified by Google Firebase Authentication.
Name and email (optional)If you enter them at sign-up or in ProfileTo personalise the app.
Searches, saved products, store visitsWhen signed inTo show your recent searches, favourites and how much you saved.
LocationOnly when you use Ride Compare and allow location accessTo fill in your pickup point and estimate fares. Not stored with your account and not used for tracking.
Pickup and drop addressesWhen you compare or book a rideTo look up the address and get fare estimates.

You can use Shop Compare and Ride Compare without an account (tap “Skip for now”). Nothing is saved to an account in that case.

Who we share it with

Only the service providers needed to run the app, and only the minimum data they need:

  • Google Firebase Authentication: your mobile number, to send and verify the OTP.
  • Google Maps Platform: the address text you type or your coordinates, to suggest and look up addresses.
  • Ride providers (e.g. Uber): pickup and drop points, to get fare estimates. When you choose a ride you continue in that provider’s own app, under its privacy policy.
  • Amazon and Flipkart: we read public product listings to compare prices. When you tap “Buy”, you continue on their website or app, under their privacy policy. We don’t send them your account details.

We may disclose data if required by law.

How long we keep it

We keep your account data until you delete your account. Search results and product prices are cached without any personal information.

Deleting your account

In the app, open the menu (☰), tap Delete account, and confirm. This permanently deletes your account, profile, recent searches, saved products, savings history and ride records from our servers and removes your sign-in. It cannot be undone.

If you can no longer open the app, request deletion through the support page and include the mobile number you signed in with.

Security

All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS). Sessions use signed tokens, and we never see or store your OTP.

Children

Tarajuu is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

Contact

Questions or requests: see the support page.
