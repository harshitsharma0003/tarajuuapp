Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?
No. Tap “Skip for now” on the sign-in screen. An account only saves your recent searches, favourites and savings.

I didn’t get the OTP.
Check that you entered your 10-digit Indian mobile number correctly, then wait 60 seconds and tap Resend. Too many requests in a short time can be blocked for about an hour.

Why does a price differ from the store?
Prices and availability change often. Tarajuu shows the latest price it found, and the store’s own page is always the final price.

Are ride fares exact?
No. They are estimates. The final fare is shown in the ride provider’s app before you book.

How do I delete my account?
Menu (☰) → Delete account → Delete. See the privacy policy for details.
